Discover the latest courses, webinars and learning opportunities. 

#### [Snowflake Platform TrainingSnowflake Platform training provides an overview of the Snowflake platform. It will explain the Snowflake AI Data Cloud architecture, while interactive labs provide guided experience in unifying, integrating, and sharing previously siloed data with virtually unlimited scalability and concurrency. You will take the training with access to a curated Snowflake account to perform handsâon labs.](https://learn.snowflake.com/en/courses/OD-SPT/)

#### [Data Science Workshop (Badge 6)Our Data Science Workshop (DSCW) introduces participants to the exciting world of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning within Snowflake. Learners will get hands-on experience with Cortex Playground, Cortex Analyst, various Large Language Models (LLMs), prompt engineering, forecasting, and classification.](https://learn.snowflake.com/en/courses/OD-ESS-DSCW/)

#### [Snowflake Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) EssentialsThis course will help you understand and adapt to Snowflake's planned move to default Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA). We'll cover why this shift is important for security, introduce new tools like the Trust Center, and show you how to use MFA policies and tools effectively. You'll also learn how to handle common issues and find resources to support you through the transition and beyond.](https://learn.snowflake.com/en/courses/OD-MFA/)

#### [Snowflake Python Data Engineering TrainingThis three-day course equips you with the expertise to integrate Python within the Snowflake AI Data Cloud. You will design and deploy high-performance data engineering solutions utilizing the Snowflake Python API and Snowpark.](https://learn.snowflake.com/en/courses/ILT-PDE/)

#### [Snowflake GenAI TrainingIn this one-day course, learners will delve into the GenAI capabilities within Snowflake. This course is designed to empower learners to integrate capabilities into their regular analytical workflows. Even without being a Data Scientist or a Machine Learning engineer, they will be able to extract insights at scale, interact with applications using natural language, drive coding productivity, and generate new content.](https://learn.snowflake.com/en/courses/ILT-GENAI/)
