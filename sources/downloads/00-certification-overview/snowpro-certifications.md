### SnowPro Specialty Certifications

The SnowPro Specialty Certification Series assesses specific Snowflake features critical to accelerating success. The series consists of three certifications: Snowpark, Native Apps, and Gen AI.

### SnowPro Advanced Certifications

The SnowPro Advanced Certifications are the Snowflake role-based exams. These exams assess advanced Snowflake skills used in specific job roles. This series consists of these certifications: Architect, Security Engineer, Data Engineer, MLOps Engineer, Data Scientist, Administrator, and Data Analyst.
